About the product

HANZO Herbal Broth is a ready-to-use Korean herbal broth made with five botanicals. Its mild, savory profile is designed for warm sipping and for use in soups, stews, noodles, rice, meat, and seafood dishes.

Both. Warm it and enjoy it as a mild savory herbal drink, or use it in place of water or stock in your favorite recipes.

It has a mild, savory, and subtly herbal character. The flavor is designed to add botanical depth without overpowering the other ingredients in a dish.

Each 1 cup (240 mL) serving has 0 calories and 130 mg of sodium. Always refer to the package Nutrition Facts and ingredient statement for the most complete and current information.

Use and storage

What Is HANZO Herbal Broth?

A premium Korean herbal cooking broth crafted to add depth and balance to meat, seafood, soups, stews, braising, and Korean-inspired dishes. Use it in place of water or stock to help reduce unwanted gamey and fishye odors while building a clean herbal flavor.

Why Choose HANZO?

Use HANZO Herbal Broth in place of water or stock to add Korean-inspired herbal depth while helping reduce unwanted gamey and fidhy odors.

  • Helps reduce unwanted gamey and fishy odors in meat, poultry, seafood, and organ meat dishes.
  • Adds clean Koean-inspired herbal depth to soups, stews, braising, steaming, ramen, rice, and grain dishes.
  • Plant-based and ready to use as a cooking broth or stock replacement.
  • Designed for modern home kirchens, specialty retail, and foodservice applications.

How to Enjoy

Swap water for HANZO in:

  • Soups & Stews
  • Steaming & Braising
  • Rice, Grains & Ramen
  • Beef, pork, poultry & seafood dishes
  • Korean-inspired home cooking

Ingredients

Water, Eleutherococcus Senticosus Root (가시오갈피), Astragalus Root (황기), Hovenia Dulcis Fruit (헛개나무), Cnidium Rhizome (천궁), Angelica Gigas Root (당귀). Contains no artificial additives, preservatives, or flavor enhancers.

Storage Instructions

Store in a cool, dry place. Refrigerate after opening and consume within 7-10 days. Do not microwave in carton.

⚠

Shake Well Before Use — Nature's Sediment: Herbal sediment is natural and perfectly safe to consume.

The Five Herbs

Chosen with Purpose.

Every ingredient earns its place — selected for depth of flavor and the culinary tradition Koreans have cherished for generations.

01

Eleutherococcus Senticosus Root

가시오갈피

Known as Siberian Ginseng, Eleutherococcus has been a prized ingredient in Korean and East Asian kitchens for centuries, valued for its earthy, robust flavor.

Earthy · Robust
02

Astragalus Root

황기

A cornerstone of traditional Korean cooking, Astragalus contributes a warm, subtly sweet depth that forms the rich backbone of our broth.

Warm · Subtly Sweet

Hovenia Dulcis Fruit

헛개나무

Mild · Naturally Sweet

Warming · Aromatic

Our Story

HANZO Herbal Broth was created to bring Korea's culinary herotage into modern everyday cooking. Inspired by traditional Korean herbal ingrefients, it is crafted as a premiun cooking broth for soups, stews, braising, meat, seafood, and Korean-inspired dishes.

Rather than positioning herbal ingredients as supplement, HANZO focuses on the kitchen - helping home cooks, chefs, retailers, and foodservice partners use Korean-inspired flavor in practical, familiar ways.

From Korean ingredient tradition to the modern American table, HANZO Herbal Broth is designed to add depth, balance, and clean herbal flavor while helping reduce unwanted gamey and fishy odors in everyday cooking.

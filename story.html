Our story

Rooted in Korea.
Created for what comes next.

Korean ingredients carry generations of culinary knowledge. Our role is to make that knowledge approachable, useful, and relevant for modern American kitchens.

Why NEXAGEHANZO

Korean character.
Everyday American ease.

NEXAGEHANZO develops and introduces Korean-inspired foods and botanical beverages for the U.S. market. We begin with familiar Korean ingredients and practical culinary knowledge, then shape them into products that are simple to understand and easy to use.

Our first featured product, HANZO Herbal Broth, reflects that approach: a ready-to-use broth made with five botanicals, designed both for warm sipping and for everyday cooking.

How we work

From product idea
to the U.S. market.

We work across product development, Korean production partnerships, U.S. import coordination, brand presentation, and channel development.

This connected approach helps preserve the character of the original ingredients while presenting each product clearly for retailers, foodservice operators, distributors, and consumers.
